Who we are

Asian-interest.
Never Asian-exclusive.

ΛΦΕ was founded in 1981 by nineteen men at UCLA who wanted to set new and higher standards of excellence for young college men — and to build something that Asian-American students could belong to without being defined by it.

That distinction still matters. Our cultural heritage is one of our five core values, and our programming reflects it. But membership has never been restricted by ethnicity, and the Alpha Xi brotherhood is stronger for it. What we look for is a man who wants to be held to a standard and who is willing to hold others to one.

Our cause

A swab takes two minutes.
It can save a life.

In 1995, Stanford brother Evan Chen was diagnosed with leukaemia. The fraternity ran what became the largest bone marrow typing drive in the history of the National Marrow Donor Program. Evan died in 1996 without finding his match.

Patients are most likely to match a donor of similar ancestry, and Asian patients remain badly underrepresented on the registry. That is why every ΛΦΕ chapter, including ours, runs a registry drive — every year, without exception.
