Retreats, families, cultural programming and interchapter trips: the parts of the fraternity that do not fit on a résumé.

The big brother programme

You don’t join alone

Every new member is paired with a big brother, an active who has been through the process and whose task is to see that you get through it too.

That pairing is no mere ceremony. Your big is the person you call about a course you’re failing, an internship application you’re stuck on, or a decision you don’t want to make alone. Over time those pairings accumulate into family lines, and the family is what most brothers mean when they say “my brothers”.

Beyond the chapter

A network you can travel on

ΛΦΕ has chapters across six regions of North America, plus associate chapters including McMaster. Interchapter visits are a real part of the calendar; brothers from Alpha Xi regularly host and are hosted in turn, and delegates go to the international convention each summer.

The practical version: you can land in a city where you know nobody and find someone who will meet you for dinner.

On campus

Part of the wider community

Alpha Xi works alongside U of T’s Asian cultural organisations and the broader Greek council. Lunar New Year events, Asian Heritage Month programming, collaborative philanthropy, and joint socials are all standing fixtures.

We are Asian-interest and open to all, and the programming reflects both.
