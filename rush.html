The process

What happens

  1. You come to rush events

    A week of open events: info night, a social, something athletic, a professional night. You meet us, we meet you. Dress is casual unless we say otherwise.

  2. Interviews

    Toward the end of rush we sit down with people properly. It is a friendly conversation, meant to understand why you’re here.

  3. Bids go out

    A bid is a formal invitation to join as a new member. Receiving one places you under no obligation. You decide.

  4. New member education

    You go through the process with your class. You learn the fraternity’s history and values, get paired with a big brother, and build the relationships that end up mattering most.

  5. You cross

    Your class crosses into the chapter together, takes a class name, and is revealed to campus. From there you’re a brother for life.
